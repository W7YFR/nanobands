An .s1p export (NanoVNA-Saver, or the NanoVNA's own SD-card save) skips calibration and tracing and gives exact data.

2 Calibrate

When a photo loads, the plot grid is found and the photo is squared up on it. If it looks off, Edit corners (above the photo) shows the 4 yellow-ringed handles; drag them onto the grid's corners (a magnifier appears while dragging), then Straighten again.

MHz

Don't know start/stop? Click a point whose frequency you know (a marker, or a VNA grid line), enter the MHz, and repeat once more. Start/stop are solved from the two.

3 Trace

Auto-trace follows the yellow line and ignores on-screen text. If it strays, turn on Pen fix and drag along the real curve to redraw that stretch. (You can also trace by hand from scratch with the pen.)

Corners moved — click Auto-trace again to re-fit.

4 Analyze

CW is legal anywhere you have privileges. The CW/data sub-bands (blue) are the parts where phone isn't allowed.

SWR
